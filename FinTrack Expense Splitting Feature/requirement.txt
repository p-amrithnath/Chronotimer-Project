FinTrack Expense Splitting Feature
Project Overview

FinTrack is a collaborative expense management application that enables users to:

Create groups
Add members
Record shared expenses
Split expenses among participants
Track balances
View settlement history
Monitor spending patterns

The goal is to simplify expense sharing among friends, roommates, families, and teams.

Business Problem

Managing shared expenses manually can lead to:

Calculation errors
Payment disputes
Lack of transparency
Difficulty tracking balances

FinTrack automates expense calculation and balance management.
